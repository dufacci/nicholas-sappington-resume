Phase 03: Nootropic & Vitamin Selection

Standard pharmaceutical prescriptions often focus solely on symptom management. Our research isolated specific vitamins (like Benfotiamine), minerals (Magnesium/Zinc Citrate), and botanicals (Alpha-Lipoic Acid, Skullcap) that actively support myelin sheath repair and mitigate oxidative damage causing neuropathy symptoms.
