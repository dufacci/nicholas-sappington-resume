A designer's approach to supplement R&D. Peripheral neuropathy — the tingling and numbness in hands and feet — is usually managed with prescriptions aimed at masking the symptom. This case study works the other direction: read the clinical literature first, find the mechanisms behind the sensation, then build a non-pharmaceutical stack of vitamins, minerals, and botanicals around them.

01

Research

Phase 01 — Neuropathy Pathology

Initial R&D focused on the biological mechanism rather than the complaint. Oxidative stress and poor microcirculation degrade the myelin sheath insulating peripheral nerves; the exposed nerve misfires, and that misfire is what a person feels as tingling or numbness. Naming the cause chain gave the formulation something concrete to target.

Phase 02 — Clinical Journals & Literature

An extensive read through the scientific literature, filtered to studies where subjects reported numbness and tingling specifically. The goal was to find metabolic pathways that pharmaceutical protocols tend to skip — nutrient deficiencies, mitochondrial energy deficits, and inflammatory signaling around irritated nerves.

Phase 03 — Nootropic & Vitamin Selection

Each candidate ingredient had to earn its place against a mechanism identified in Phase 01. That produced three primary actives — Alpha-Lipoic Acid, Benfotiamine, Acetyl-L-Carnitine — supported by minerals for conduction, antioxidants for oxidative load, and botanicals for inflammation and circulation. Nothing is in the stack for label appeal.

    03

    Outcome

    Mechanism-first

    Every active traces back to a documented pathway from the research phase, so the label can be defended line by line rather than justified after the fact.

    Format for adherence

    A gummy delivery format was chosen deliberately — the best formulation is worthless if the customer stops taking it in week two.

    Sourcing & purity

    Global ingredient sourcing managed against efficacy and purity requirements, with forms specified (citrate, benfotiamine) rather than left to the manufacturer.
